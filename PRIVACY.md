# Privacy policy

**3 Patti Chips Handler** · effective 2 October 2026

3 Patti Chips Handler keeps track of chips while friends play cards together. It works only between
phones on the same WiFi or hotspot. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it
never sends anything to the developer or to any server on the internet.

## What the app keeps on your phone

- The name you type, so you don't have to type it again.
- A random ID made by the app, so the host can give you back your seat when you reconnect.
- The address of the last table you joined, and whether you like the Table or List view.
- On the host's phone: the open table, which means the names players typed, their chips, and the
  history of rounds and moves. It is kept so the host can resume after closing the app.

Players who join from a browser keep the same kind of data (their name and a random ID) in that
browser's storage.

## What the app sends, and to whom

While you play, the phones at the same table send each other the table: player names, chips, bets
and moves. This travels only over your local WiFi or the host's hotspot, directly between the phones
at that table. Nothing is sent over the internet, and the developer never receives any of it.

## Permissions

- **Network and WiFi**: to host a table and to find and join tables on your WiFi or hotspot.
- **Notifications and running in the background**: the host's phone shows a notification and keeps
  the table running while the screen is off, so other players stay connected.

The app doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Deleting your data

Delete a saved table from the home screen, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove
everything. Browser players can clear the site's data in their browser.

## Children

The app is meant for adults playing card games together. It is not directed at children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions can be asked
by opening an issue at https://github.com/itz-puneet/3-patti_local/issues.
